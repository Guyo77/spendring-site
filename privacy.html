Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 19, 2026

Atenri is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect a financial account through Plaid, we may access transaction data, account balances, and limited account metadata necessary to provide the app’s functionality.

We never receive or store your banking usernames or passwords.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Calculate and display daily spending activity
  • Generate spending insights and reports
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Provide customer support

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal financial data.

Information may be shared only with trusted third-party providers required to operate the app, including Plaid and secure infrastructure providers.

4. Security

Data is transmitted using industry-standard encryption technologies, including HTTPS and TLS. While we take reasonable measures to protect information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary to provide and improve the service. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Your Rights

You may disconnect linked financial accounts or request deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Third-Party Services

Financial connections are powered by Plaid. Use of Plaid is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Atenri is not intended for children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
